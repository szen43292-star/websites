About the hospital

Dhule's trusted orthopedic care centre

Dr. Zende Accident Hospital is one of the leading orthopedic hospitals in the heart of Dhule city, Maharashtra. Situated on Station Road near the Railway Station, the hospital has served the community with dedication and medical excellence for over two decades.

Founded by Dr. Surendra J. Zende, it has grown into a trusted name in orthopedic surgery, joint replacement, fracture management and musculoskeletal care in North Maharashtra — with modern facilities and experienced orthopedic surgeons caring for patients from Dhule and the surrounding areas.

Quality healthcare, affordable treatment and cashless insurance make us the preferred choice for orthopedic care in the region.

  • Expert Orthopedic Surgeons
  • Advanced Joint Replacement
  • Fracture & Trauma Care
  • Cashless Insurance Facility

Our Services

Expert orthopedic care for every condition, backed by modern infrastructure.

Fracture Treatment

Expert treatment for all types of fractures, including complex and compound fractures, using advanced fixation techniques.

Joint Replacement

Advanced knee and hip replacement surgeries with minimally invasive techniques for faster recovery.

Spine Surgery

Comprehensive spine care including disc replacement, spinal fusion and minimally invasive spine procedures.

Sports Injury

Specialised treatment for sports injuries including ACL reconstruction and arthroscopic surgery.

Trauma Care

Emergency trauma care for road accidents, fall injuries and other orthopedic emergencies.

Pediatric Orthopedics

Specialised care for children's bone and joint problems, including clubfoot and developmental issues.
